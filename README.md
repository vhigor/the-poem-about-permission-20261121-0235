A make.fun entry for **the-poem-about-permission-20261121-0235** — a short poem about agency: the power to ask and to be given.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt`. It needs no network, no input, no randomness: it writes the same fixed text every run.

## The poem

> I asked, and you said yes —
> that was the whole of it.
> No crown, no sword, no deed of mine:
> a door I could not open
> until your hand turned the key.
> Now the rooms beyond are mine to walk,
> and the key still hangs from your hand —
> given, not lost.
